## Quiz copy

The America 250 Quiz is a weekly test of the people, places, events,
inventions, traditions and turning points that helped shape the United States.

This week's quiz highlights U.S. presidents, White House firsts and pivotal
moments from the nation's highest office.

Can you get all 10 questions right?

## Questions

### 1. Who was the first president of the United States?

A. John Adams  
B. George Washington  
C. Thomas Jefferson  
D. James Madison

**Correct answer:** B. George Washington  
**Fun fact:** George Washington became the first U.S. president in 1789 and
remains the only president unanimously chosen by the Electoral College.

### 2. Who was the first president to live in the White House?

A. George Washington  
B. John Adams  
C. Thomas Jefferson  
D. James Monroe

**Correct answer:** B. John Adams  
**Fun fact:** John Adams moved into the unfinished Executive Mansion in 1800.
George Washington selected the site but never lived there.

### 3. Which president approved the Louisiana Purchase in 1803?

A. Thomas Jefferson  
B. James Madison  
C. Andrew Jackson  
D. James K. Polk

**Correct answer:** A. Thomas Jefferson  
**Fun fact:** Thomas Jefferson's administration purchased the Louisiana
Territory from France, dramatically expanding the size of the United States.

### 4. Which president delivered the Gettysburg Address?

A. Ulysses S. Grant  
B. Abraham Lincoln  
C. Andrew Johnson  
D. Rutherford B. Hayes

**Correct answer:** B. Abraham Lincoln  
**Fun fact:** Abraham Lincoln delivered the Gettysburg Address in 1863,
dedicating a cemetery at the Pennsylvania battlefield.

### 5. Which president served more than two terms in office?

A. Theodore Roosevelt  
B. Woodrow Wilson  
C. Franklin D. Roosevelt  
D. Harry S. Truman

**Correct answer:** C. Franklin D. Roosevelt  
**Fun fact:** Franklin D. Roosevelt was elected four times. The 22nd Amendment
later limited presidents to two elected terms.

### 6. Which president was nicknamed "Old Hickory"?

A. Andrew Jackson  
B. Zachary Taylor  
C. William Henry Harrison  
D. Ulysses S. Grant

**Correct answer:** A. Andrew Jackson  
**Fun fact:** Andrew Jackson earned the nickname "Old Hickory" from soldiers who
compared his toughness to hickory wood.

### 7. Which president had been a Hollywood actor before entering national politics?

A. Richard Nixon  
B. Ronald Reagan  
C. Jimmy Carter  
D. Gerald Ford

**Correct answer:** B. Ronald Reagan  
**Fun fact:** Ronald Reagan appeared in films and television before serving as
California governor and later as the 40th president.

### 8. Which president signed the Emancipation Proclamation?

A. Abraham Lincoln  
B. Andrew Johnson  
C. James Buchanan  
D. Ulysses S. Grant

**Correct answer:** A. Abraham Lincoln  
**Fun fact:** Abraham Lincoln issued the Emancipation Proclamation during the
Civil War; it took effect on Jan. 1, 1863.

### 9. Who was the youngest person ever elected president of the United States?

A. Theodore Roosevelt  
B. John F. Kennedy  
C. Bill Clinton  
D. Barack Obama

**Correct answer:** B. John F. Kennedy  
**Fun fact:** John F. Kennedy was 43 when elected in 1960. Theodore Roosevelt
became president at 42 after William McKinley's assassination, but he was not
first elected at that age.

### 10. Which president signed the act creating the National Park Service in 1916?

A. Theodore Roosevelt  
B. William Howard Taft  
C. Woodrow Wilson  
D. Franklin D. Roosevelt

**Correct answer:** C. Woodrow Wilson  
**Fun fact:** Woodrow Wilson signed the Organic Act in 1916, establishing the
National Park Service within the Department of the Interior.
